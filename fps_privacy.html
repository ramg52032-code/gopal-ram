Privacy Policy

Last updated: 06 February, 2025

FPS Basics is an educational application designed to help beginners understand the fundamentals of first-person shooter games. Your privacy is important, and this policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information Collection and Use

FPS Basics does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data.

The app:

  • does not require account creation
  • does not request user information
  • does not track usage or behavior
  • does not send or receive data over the internet

All content is available entirely offline.

Permissions

FPS Basics does not request any device permissions, including internet access.

Third-Party Services

FPS Basics does not use any third-party services, including:

  • analytics tools
  • advertising networks
  • crash reporting services
  • external APIs or SDKs

No data is shared with third parties because no data is collected.

Data Security

Since FPS Basics does not collect or store any personal information, there is no risk of personal data being accessed, leaked, or misused through the app.

Children’s Privacy

FPS Basics is an educational app and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or users of any age. Because no data is collected, the app is safe for general audiences.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app’s functionality changes in the future in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated accordingly. Any changes will be reflected on the page where this policy is published.

Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may reach us via info@gopalram.online.
